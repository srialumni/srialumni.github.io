Zoom Information for the 2026 Annual Reunion

Thursday September 17, 2026, 5:15 - 6:15 pm PT / 8:15 - 9:15 pm ET

SRI has provided a Zoom link for alumni association members to watch SRI CEO David Parekh's report on SRI, as well as the association's Hall of Fame awards, at theSRI Alumni Reunion September 17. The zoom option is offered to Alumni association members who have renewed in the past year. If your membership has lapsed, renew today.

The Zoom portion of the reunion program will start around 5:15 pm PT / 8:15 pm ET and run approximately one hour.

Zoom Link: https://sri.zoomgov.com/j/1608097872

Program (Zoom portion):

  • Report on SRI by SRI CEO David Parekh
  • Hall of Fame induction honoring James Barnum, Barbara Grosz, and Jon Mirsalis, plus Center Spotlight: SRI AIC

While we can't share the preceeding social hour or food and drink virtually, we hope that Alumni Association members who can’t attend in person can join in Zoom!
